Airplane Chime Timer

Authentic aircraft chimes for focus sessions, breaks, medication reminders, game timing, and any routine that works better with sound instead of popups.

1. Pin it Keep the extension visible so the timer is one click away.
2. Pick a mode Use a single interval, a specific time, or dual timers with different sounds.
3. Turn it on The badge changes to ON and the next chime time is shown in the popup.
  • Timer is OFF by default, so nothing plays until you enable it.
  • Single Timer Mode: Plays one chime at regular intervals such as 15min, 30min, or 1 hour.
  • Dual Timer Mode: Plays two different chimes with overlap priority, so hourly reminders can feel different from short breaks.
  • Set custom intervals (any minute value) for more flexible timing
  • Schedule chimes at specific times of day with daily repeat option
  • No popup notifications and no tracking - audio only, stored locally in Chrome
  • Chimes exactly at :00 seconds of each interval
  • Upload and use your own custom chime sounds (MP3, WAV up to 1MB)
  • Smart interval validation ensures dual timers work harmoniously together

🔥 New: Dual Timer Example

Setup: Timer 1 every 15 minutes with "Ding" sound, Timer 2 every 60 minutes with "Dong" sound
Result: 09:15 Ding, 09:30 Ding, 09:45 Ding, 10:00 Dong (priority), 10:15 Ding, 10:30 Ding, 10:45 Ding, 11:00 Dong (priority)...
Useful when you want short focus cues and stronger hour marks without visual interruptions.
