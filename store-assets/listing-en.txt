Keep track of time with a familiar airplane-style chime. Airplane Chime Timer is a Chrome audio reminder for hourly cues, break reminders and everyday routines, without popup notifications.

Set a reminder that fits your day
- Hourly or repeating reminders: choose every 15 minutes, 30 minutes or hour, or enter a custom interval from 1 to 1,440 minutes.
- A specific time: schedule one sound cue, or repeat it daily.
- Dual timers: give short and long intervals different sounds. The longer interval must be a multiple of the shorter one. When both are due together, the longer timer takes priority.
- Check the actual next scheduled reminder in the extension popup. Close the popup after setup and keep Chrome running.

Make the sound your own
- Preview the included airplane-style sounds before turning the timer on.
- Adjust the volume or mute it. Sounds vary in intensity, so preview your choice and use a lower volume if you prefer a softer cue.
- Add your own audio file up to 1 MB, such as MP3 or WAV. Playback depends on the audio formats supported by Chrome.

Start in three steps
1. Open Airplane Chime Timer from Chrome's Extensions menu. Pin it for easy access.
2. Choose your sound, volume and timing. Test the sound while the timer is OFF.
3. Turn Timer Active on and check the next scheduled chime.
The setup guide and status guidance are available in English and Korean. Main timer controls are in English.

For a 25-minute focus cue, choose Custom interval and enter 25. This repeats one interval; it does not automatically alternate 25-minute focus and 5-minute break sessions.

No account. No tracking.
Timer settings and custom audio stay in Chrome local storage. The extension has no product analytics and does not collect browsing history.

Before you rely on a reminder
Keep Chrome running and your computer awake. Sleep, browser shutdown or system scheduling can delay or prevent playback. Exact second timing is not guaranteed. Use this for everyday reminders, not safety-critical alarms.
