Airplane Chime Timer brings familiar airplane-style chimes to everyday reminders. Use a gentle sound cue to notice the hour, pause for a break, or return to your routine without a popup notification.

Choose a reminder:
- Every 15 or 30 minutes, every hour, or a custom interval from 1 to 1,440 minutes.
- A specific time of day, once or repeated daily.
- Two intervals with different sounds. The longer timer takes priority when both are due together.

Choose the sound:
- Preview the included chimes before turning the timer on.
- Adjust the volume, including mute. The included sounds vary in intensity; use a quieter chime and lower volume for a gentle reminder.
- Add your own audio file up to 1 MB, such as MP3 or WAV. Playback depends on Chrome's supported audio formats.
- Check the actual next scheduled reminder in the popup.

Start in three steps:
1. Open Airplane Chime Timer from Chrome's Extensions menu and pin it if you like.
2. Choose your sound, volume, and timing. Test the sound while the timer is OFF.
3. Turn Timer Active on and check the scheduled chime.
An English/Korean setup guide is available from the popup.

For a 25-minute focus cue, choose Custom interval and enter 25. This repeats one interval; it does not automatically alternate 25-minute focus and 5-minute break sessions.

Keep Chrome running and your computer awake. The popup can be closed after setup. Sleep, browser shutdown, or system scheduling can delay or prevent playback; exact second timing is not guaranteed. This is an everyday reminder, not a safety-critical alarm.

No account is required, and no product analytics are collected. Timer settings and uploaded sounds are stored locally in Chrome. The extension does not send browsing history, uploaded audio, or timer activity to an analytics service.
